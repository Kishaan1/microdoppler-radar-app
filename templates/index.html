Theoretical Principles & Micro-Doppler Physics

Doppler Shift Equation

f_d = (2 · v · f_c) / c

Relates target radial velocity ($v$) and carrier frequency ($f_c = 24.125\text{ GHz}$) to received frequency shift.

STFT Spectrogram Analysis

S(t,f) = |∫ x(τ)w(τ-t)e^(-j2πfτ)dτ|²

Computes 64 frequency magnitude channels over sliding temporal windows ($N_{\text{fft}} = 64$) at 10 Hz.

Micro-Motion Modulation

Δf_micro ∝ f_rot · r_blade

Captures secondary micro-motions (rotor blade rotation, wingbeats, gait cycles) unique to each target class.
